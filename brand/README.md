# Akea brand — Stone & Forest

Source of the logo and icons used by the website, the suite (/app) and the phone app.

- Wordmark: "akea" in Cormorant Garamond Italic, weight 300, letter-spacing -0.03em, with a bronze diamond.
  Traced to vector outlines (with the font's kerning) by `make_brand.py`, so it looks the same everywhere.
- Colours: forest #14241F (ink, dark surfaces) · bronze #9C7A43 (accent on light) · gold #C9A66B (accent on dark)
  · stone #E4DED3 · paper #F5F2EC · cream #EDE6D8 (text on dark).
- Type: Cormorant Garamond Italic (display) and Jost (text), both SIL Open Font License; served from our own domain.

Rebuild the SVGs: `python3 make_brand.py "CormorantGaramond-Italic[wght].ttf" out`
